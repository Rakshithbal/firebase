# 13 · 5 voice-over scripts for women creators: rooftop B-roll with VO

**Bi Ki BOO presents #OPEN AIR · Regenta rooftop, Airport Road · Sat 5 Dec · Bullzeye**
These follow R1–R10 in [11](11-rooftop-scripts-women.md) and [12](12-rooftop-scripts-women-set2.md), with the same brief, specs and rules: 9:16, warm sunset grade, the end frame *"#OPEN AIR · 05.12 · Regenta Rooftop"*, and the Paid partnership label plus **#ad** or **#collab** on every post. They never say "book", "tickets", "buy" or a price, and never show bottles, labels or Terrace guests' faces.

**What's different here:** the voice carries the video. The creator is mostly *not* talking to camera. She's in the shots, living the rooftop, while her voice tells the story over the top. That lets you use the most cinematic B-roll from both shoot days.

| # | Title | Voices | Length | VO style | Rung it moves |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R11 | **"Observed in the wild"** | Solo | 30 s | Hushed nature documentary | Solution: it exists here |
| R12 | **"Dear Hubballi"** | Solo | 35 s | Spoken-word letter to the city | Problem and cause |
| R13 | **"This is your cabin crew"** | Solo | 25 s | Airline cabin announcement | Mechanism: what the night is |
| R14 | **"What if"** | Duo | 30 s | Her anxious thoughts, answered by her calm friend | Risk: objections answered |
| R15 | **"Remember Saturday?"** | Trio | 35 s | Three friends remembering a night that hasn't happened yet | Action: future nostalgia |

## How to record the VO (applies to all five)
- **Record the voice first, then cut the pictures to it.** Pace: about 2.3 words per second. The word counts below fit the lengths.
- **Phone mic is fine** if you record in a closet or under a blanket, about 15 cm from the mouth. No echo, no fan.
- **Duck the music** to around −18 dB under the voice, and let it swell only in the gaps marked *(beat)*.
- **Captions:** burn in the VO word for word, in short lines. Most people watch without sound.
- **The first 8 seconds:** in every script, the opening line works even with the sound off, because the caption carries it.

## R11 · "Observed in the wild" · Solo · 30 s · ~70 words
**Job:** show this kind of night exists here, wrapped in a joke people will share. **Before:** "Nothing like this happens in Hubballi." **After:** "Apparently it does, and it's rare enough to be documented." **Frame:** identity plus humour.
**Delivery:** a hushed, reverent nature-documentary whisper, slow and very serious. The comedy comes from the gap between the tone and what's on screen. Don't imitate any real presenter's voice.

**Hooks written (top 2 kept ★):**
1. ★ *"Here, above Airport Road, we observe something rarely seen in Hubballi."* (pattern interrupt + curiosity)
2. ★ The caption *"a nature documentary about my friends"* over a slow-motion hair flip (format callout)
3. *"For years, scientists believed she only existed in Bengaluru."* (contrarian)
4. *"Do not disturb her. She is watching the sunset."* (humour)
5. *"Episode one: the Hubballi girl, at golden hour."* (series format)

| Time | B-roll | VO |
|---|---|---|
| 0–4 s | **Hook.** A long lens through the plants: the creator at the railing, sun behind her, very still, like wildlife. | *"Here, above Airport Road, we observe something rarely seen in Hubballi."* |
| 4–8 s | A slow zoom in as she turns her head. | *"The Hubballi girl. Out after sunset. And not in Bengaluru."* |
| 8–13 s | Close-up: she unfolds a shawl and wraps it round her shoulders. | *"She begins her ritual. First, the shawl. It is sixteen degrees. She has prepared."* |
| 13–18 s | Two friends arrive and the three hug. | *"Others of her kind arrive. The group forms."* |
| 18–22 s | A quick burst of them taking photos of each other. | *"They take four hundred photographs. This is normal."* |
| 22–27 s | The sun touches the horizon, the rig lights come on and all three turn towards the sound. | *"And as the light goes… the sound begins."* *(beat)* |
| 27–30 s | Back to the long lens, her silhouette. End frame. | *"Experts believe she will return. On Saturday."* |

**Caption:** *"#collab Episode 1. Rarely observed. Never in Bengaluru."*
**Shoot:** Day B (3 Dec), because it needs the rig lights. Use a long lens or 2× zoom from behind the plants so it really looks like a wildlife film.

## R12 · "Dear Hubballi" · Solo · 35 s · ~78 words
**Job:** name what Hubballi women have gone without, tenderly, without blaming anyone. **Before:** "This city wasn't built for nights like this." **After:** "It finally is." **Frame:** identity and belonging. This is the emotional anchor of the whole set.
**Delivery:** spoken word. Slow, warm and close to the mic, with real pauses at each line break. No music for the first 4 seconds. Let the first line sit in silence.

**Hooks written:**
1. ★ In silence, over a black screen fading up to the skyline: *"Dear Hubballi."* (pattern interrupt: silence and a direct address)
2. ★ The caption *"a letter to the city that raised me"* over her back, facing the sunset (callout + emotion)
3. *"You gave me everything. Except this."* (open loop)
4. *"Hubballi, we need to talk."* (curiosity; too breakup-like for this tone)
5. *"Every girl who grew up here will understand this."* (identity callout; save it for the caption)

| Time | B-roll | VO |
|---|---|---|
| 0–4 s | **Hook.** Black, then a slow fade up on the skyline from behind her. Silence. | *"Dear Hubballi."* *(beat)* |
| 4–10 s | Archive-style phone clips (her own): a school gate, girmit at a stall, rain on a scooter. | *"You gave me school runs and Sunday dosas. Girmit in the rain. And a curfew I knew by heart."* |
| 10–14 s | Back on the rooftop: she walks slowly towards the railing. Music starts very softly. | *"You never gave me this."* |
| 14–20 s | A wide shot: the sky on fire, the whole city below. | *"A sky this wide. This close to home."* |
| 20–27 s | Her two friends join her at the railing, arms linked. The bass from soundcheck is faintly audible. | *"A song I can feel in my chest. My best friends on either side of me."* |
| 27–31 s | Close on her phone: a message from Amma that says *"Enjoy 🤍"*. | *"And a text from Amma that just says: enjoy."* |
| 31–35 s | Her silhouette as the sun disappears. End frame. | *"This Saturday, you finally do. Love… your girl."* |

**Caption:** *"#ad For every girl who grew up here."*
**Note:** the Amma message should be a real one (ask her mother to send it), not a mock-up. The archive clips must be the creator's own footage.

## R13 · "This is your cabin crew" · Solo · 25 s · ~62 words
**Job:** explain what the night actually is (sunset, the cold, Bullzeye, the view) in a format that sticks. It plays on Airport Road and on C03's "Gate: Rooftop" boarding pass. **Before:** "I don't know what this event even is." **After:** "Sunset, Bullzeye, sixteen degrees. Got it." **Frame:** simplicity plus fun.
**Delivery:** a bright, crisp cabin-crew voice, slightly too formal. Add a soft *"ding"* chime before the first line. Don't name or imitate any real airline.

**Hooks written:**
1. ★ *Ding.* *"Good evening, and welcome aboard your flight to Saturday."* (pattern interrupt: a familiar sound in the wrong place)
2. ★ The caption *"Gate: Rooftop · Boarding 5 pm"* over the lift doors opening (specificity + curiosity)
3. *"Cabin crew, prepare for golden hour."* (format)
4. *"This flight does not leave Hubballi."* (contrarian)
5. *"Your captain tonight is a national act."* (stakes; too much of a reveal for the hook)

| Time | B-roll | VO |
|---|---|---|
| 0–4 s | **Hook.** *Ding.* POV from the lift as the doors slide open onto the rooftop, like a cabin door. A plane passes overhead in the distance (time it). | *"Good evening, and welcome aboard your flight to Saturday."* |
| 4–8 s | She walks out holding the black boarding-pass invite. | *"We're departing Hubballi at golden hour. Cruising altitude: rooftop."* |
| 8–12 s | Her hand points out the skyline, cabin-crew style, two fingers. | *"Please note the view on both sides of the aircraft."* |
| 12–16 s | She pulls on a shawl. | *"Temperature at destination: sixteen degrees. Keep your shawl within reach."* |
| 16–20 s | The soundcheck: the booth (with consent), the bass hits and she grins. | *"Your captain this evening is Bullzeye."* |
| 20–23 s | Her phone filming the sunset, battery at 100%. | *"Please make sure your phone is fully charged."* |
| 23–25 s | She looks into the lens and smiles. End frame. | *"Cabin crew, doors to manual. See you at Gate: Rooftop."* |

**Caption:** *"#collab Now boarding. 05.12 🛫"*
**Shoot:** Day B (3 Dec), for the soundcheck shot. Recording the plane passing over is optional, but worth trying.

## R14 · "What if" · Duo · 30 s · ~65 words, two voices
**Job:** answer the real objections out loud, ending on the one nobody admits to: fear of missing out on her own city. **Before:** "Too many things could go wrong." **After:** "Every one has an answer. The only real risk is staying home." **Frame:** risk removal, then loss.
**Delivery:** **Voice A** (the creator) is her inner voice: fast, slightly breathless, overthinking. **Voice B** (her friend) is calm, flat and short, almost bored. The comedy is in B's two-word answers. Record them together so the timing is real.

**Hooks written:**
1. ★ Voice A, fast: *"Okay but what if it's too late, what if Amma says no, what if I don't know anyone—"* Voice B: *"You know me."* (pattern interrupt + relatability)
2. ★ The caption *"my brain vs. my best friend"* over the two of them at the railing (format callout)
3. *"Every overthinker needs this friend."* (callout; save it for the caption)
4. *"I almost didn't go because of 6 'what ifs'."* (open loop)
5. *"Things I worried about vs. what actually happened."* (contrast; claims an outcome that hasn't happened yet)

| Time | B-roll | VO |
|---|---|---|
| 0–5 s | **Hook.** A close-up of A, chewing her lip, looking out at the sunset. B leans on the railing beside her, scrolling her phone. | **A:** *"Okay, but what if it's too late, what if Amma says no, what if I don't know anyone—"* **B:** *"You know me."* |
| 5–9 s | A pulls her arms in against the wind. B, without looking up, drops a shawl over her shoulders. | **A:** *"What if it's cold—"* **B:** *"Shawl. Packed."* |
| 9–13 s | A glances at her phone clock. B points over her shoulder at the help desk and the **[cab partner]** sign. | **A:** *"What if I can't get home—"* **B:** *"Cab desk. Right there."* |
| 13–17 s | A looks at her heels. B holds up a pair of flats from her bag. | **A:** *"What if my feet—"* **B:** *"Flats."* |
| 17–22 s | The rig lights come on. A goes quiet and turns to look at them. Music swells. | **A** (slower now): *"What if… it's actually really good?"* |
| 22–27 s | A close-up of A's face lit orange, half smiling. | **A:** *"And I've been staying home all this time for nothing?"* *(beat)* |
| 27–30 s | B finally looks up from her phone, at her. End frame. | **B:** *"…Yeah. That's the scary one."* |

**Caption:** *"#collab Tag the friend who answers all your what-ifs."*
**Why it works:** it lists four real objections (getting home late, the cold, the ride home, shoes) and answers each in two words. Then it turns the fear around: the real risk is *missing it*. It works as a loss frame because the "loss" is real and specific, not invented urgency.

## R15 · "Remember Saturday?" · Trio · 35 s · ~80 words, three voices
**Job:** make the viewer feel the memory before the night happens, so going feels like the natural next step. **Before:** "Maybe I'll go." **After:** "I want to be in that memory." **Frame:** future and social. Future nostalgia is the format.
**Delivery:** three friends lying back on loungers after the shoot, recorded **as one real, relaxed conversation**, overlapping and laughing. Past tense throughout, as if it's already happened. The twist is the last line.

**Hooks written:**
1. ★ Voice 1, nostalgic: *"Remember Saturday? The sun went down right behind the stage."* (open loop: the past tense is confusing in a good way)
2. ★ The caption *"us, talking about the best night of our lives"* over three friends lying on loungers looking up (curiosity + social)
3. *"You cried at the sunset." "I did not." "She did."* (cold-open banter)
4. *"Best night Hubballi ever had. We were there."* (claims an outcome before it happens)
5. *"Three of us. One rooftop. One night we'll never stop talking about."* (format; a bit generic)

| Time | B-roll | VO |
|---|---|---|
| 0–5 s | **Hook.** Overhead: three friends lying back on loungers, heads together, the sky above them. | **V1:** *"Remember Saturday? The sun went down right behind the stage."* |
| 5–9 s | A slow-motion shot of the sun behind the booth. | **V2:** *"And then the drums. I felt them in my teeth."* |
| 9–14 s | Dollu kunitha drummers in rehearsal (3 Dec, with consent), then a cut to V3 laughing. | **V3:** *"You cried at the sunset."* **V1:** *"I did* not*."* **V2:** *"She did."* |
| 14–20 s | The headphone rack glowing. One of them puts a pair on; the others watch. | **V3:** *"Then ten o'clock happened. And none of us could even talk about it."* |
| 20–25 s | The three of them walking to the lift, shawls on, arm in arm. | **V1:** *"We were home by [close time]. Amma was still awake."* **V2:** *"Asking for videos."* |
| 25–30 s | Back to the overhead shot on the loungers. A pause. | **V3:** *"…We should go back."* |
| 30–33 s | V1 lifts her head and looks at the other two. | **V1:** *"Wait."* *(beat)* *"It hasn't happened yet."* |
| 33–35 s | All three burst out laughing. End frame. | Music swells. Caption: *"It hasn't happened yet."* → end frame |

**Caption:** *"#ad Saturday, already our favourite memory. Who's in it with us?"* The +3 squad pass code goes in the Story sticker.
**Honesty check:** the whole piece is clearly imagined (the last line says so), but every detail in it must be **booked and real**: drums, sunset behind the stage, the 10 pm moment, the close time. If anything changes, re-record that line.
