## R6 · "Interview with the sun" · Solo · 25 s
**Job:** show the experience exists here, and that the sunset is the opening act. **Before:** "Sunsets are just sunsets." **After:** "This one is an event, and I need to be up there before 6." **Frame:** opportunity and wonder.

**Hooks written (top 2 kept ★):**
1. ★ She holds a mic up to the setting sun: *"First question. How long have you been doing this?"* (pattern interrupt)
2. ★ *"I got an exclusive interview with the most famous thing in Hubballi."* (curiosity + specificity)
3. *"Nobody in Hubballi watches you. Does that hurt?"* (emotional open loop)
4. *"The sun agreed to one interview before Saturday."* (stakes)
5. *"POV: you're the sun's journalist."* (format callout)

| Time | Shot | Audio / line | Sun's reply (on-screen text, typed out like a chat) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–3 s | **Hook.** Over her shoulder: she holds a small handheld mic, very serious, pointed at the sun just above the skyline. | *"First question. How long have you been doing this?"* | *"Every evening. Since forever."* |
| 3–8 s | Close on her nodding like a journalist. | *"And who usually watches you?"* | *"Mostly people stuck at the Airport Road signal."* |
| 8–13 s | A wide shot: just her and the sun on the empty rooftop. Softer now. | *"When did a girl last watch you from up here?"* | *"…Never."* |
| 13–18 s | She leans in, conspiratorial. | *"I hear Saturday's different."* | *"Someone's playing music for me. STOKED, I think."* |
| 18–22 s | The sun touches the horizon and she checks her watch. | *"Any advice for people coming?"* | *"Get here before 6. I don't wait for anyone."* |
| 22–25 s | The sun disappears. She lowers the mic and speaks quietly to camera. End frame. | *"…Iconic."* | *"#OPEN AIR · 05.12 · Regenta Rooftop"* |

**Caption:** *"#collab Exclusive. The sun doesn't do interviews. Arrive before 6 on the 5th 🌅"*
**Real urgency:** the sunset time is a true deadline. **Check the 5 Dec sunset time for Hubballi** (around 6 pm) and make sure the doors open before it.

## R7 · "What's in my rooftop bag" · Solo · 30 s
**Job:** answer "I don't know how to do this night". Each item quietly handles a worry. **Before:** "A night out is complicated: cold, shoes, getting home." **After:** "It's one small bag. I'm sorted." **Frame:** simplicity.

**Hooks written:**
1. ★ She tips a tiny bag upside down on a rooftop table: *"Six things. That's the whole night."* (specificity)
2. ★ *"Packing for Hubballi's first rooftop night. Number 4 is for my mother."* (open loop + callout)
3. *"What I'm carrying up eight floors on Saturday."* (curiosity; only say "eight" if the floor count is true)
4. *"Things you don't need on a rooftop, and the 6 you do."* (contrarian)
5. *"My rooftop bag, rated by my dad."* (relatable; needs a second person)

| Time | Shot | Audio / line | On-screen text |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–4 s | **Hook.** Top-down on a small black bag on a rooftop table, the sunset at the edge of frame. She tips it out. | *"Six things. That's the whole night."* | *"what's in my rooftop bag"* |
| 4–8 s | Item 1: a folded shawl, flicked open, catching the wind. | *"One. It's 16 degrees up here. Shawl, always."* | *"1 · shawl"* |
| 8–12 s | Item 2: a pair of flats next to her heels. She points at the heels and shakes her head. | *"Two. Flats in the bag. My feet have plans."* | *"2 · flats"* |
| 12–16 s | Item 3: a power bank, then her pass held up to the sky. | *"Three. Phone at a hundred, because I'll be filming the whole sunset."* | *"3 · power bank + pass"* |
| 16–21 s | Item 4: her phone screen showing *"Sharing live location with Amma."* | *"Four. Live location to Amma. Non-negotiable."* | *"4 · for Amma"* |
| 21–25 s | Item 5: the cab app open, then she points off-screen at the help desk and **[cab partner]** sign. | *"Five. Cab's sorted. They have a desk right there."* | *"5 · ride home"* |
| 25–28 s | Item 6: a lipstick. She puts it on in the phone's reflection and the sun hits her face. | *"Six. Obviously."* | *"6 · sunset lip"* |
| 28–30 s | End frame. | — | *"#OPEN AIR · 05.12 · Regenta Rooftop"* |

**Caption:** *"#ad Save this for Saturday. What's your item 7?"*
**Why it works:** it's saveable and practical, and the two safety items (4 and 5) slip in between shoes and lipstick, so it never feels like a lecture.

## R8 · "One star" · Solo · 20 s
**Job:** make the night seem better than anything else in the city, using a joke review. **Before:** "It'll be like every other 'party' in town." **After:** "Apparently it ruins everything else." **Frame:** contrarian, then identity.

**Hooks written:**
1. ★ Straight to camera, deadpan: *"One star. I'm never going anywhere else in Hubballi again."* (contrarian + pattern interrupt)
2. ★ The text *"⭐ 1/5 · would not recommend"* over a stunning lights-on shot (contradiction)
3. *"My honest review of a rooftop I saw for 20 minutes."* (curiosity)
4. *"I'm angry at a rooftop."* (pattern interrupt)
5. *"Don't go. You'll ruin your weekends forever."* (reverse psychology; too pushy, so I dropped it)

| Time | Shot | Audio / line | On-screen text |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–3 s | **Hook.** Tight on her face, deadpan, the rig's lights flickering on behind her. | *"One star. I'm never going anywhere else in Hubballi again."* | *"⭐ 1/5"* |
| 3–8 s | Quick cuts as she lists her "complaints": the sweeping skyline, | *"The view was too much. I couldn't look at my phone."* | *"− too much view"* |
| 8–12 s | the soundcheck bass hit making her jump, | *"The sound check alone ruined my car speakers for me."* | *"− ruined my car speakers"* |
| 12–16 s | the lights coming up as she spins once, against her will. | *"And I danced. At a sound check. In front of people."* | *"− made me dance"* |
| 16–18 s | Back to deadpan. | *"One star. Even the cab home was boring. No drama at all. See you Saturday."* | — |
| 18–20 s | End frame. | — | *"#OPEN AIR · 05.12 · Regenta Rooftop"* |

**Caption:** *"#collab Worst review I've ever written."*
**Ethics:** it's an obvious joke, posted only as a Reel on her own account with the paid label. **Never post it as a review on Google, Zomato or anywhere else**, because that would be a fake review.

## R9 · "My friend can't take photos" · Duo · 25 s
**Job:** social proof and tags. The rooftop makes everyone look good. **Before:** "I'll go if my friend goes." **After:** "We're going, and even *she* will get good photos." **Frame:** social and identity.

**Cast:** A (the poser) and B (the famously terrible photographer, played with love).

**Hooks written:**
1. ★ *"My best friend has taken 400 photos of me. This is the best one."* A blurry photo with a thumb in the corner. (specificity + humour)
2. ★ A rapid montage of awful photos (cut-off heads, thumbs, closed eyes) with the text *"tag the friend who takes your photos like this"* (callout)
3. *"I found the one place where her photos come out good."* (open loop)
4. *"She can't take photos. The sun can."* (contrast)
5. *"Rating my friend's photography in every place in Hubballi."* (format)

| Time | Shot | Audio / line | On-screen text |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–4 s | **Hook.** A holds up her phone to camera: a blurry photo, a thumb in the corner, her eyes shut. | A: *"My best friend has taken 400 photos of me. This is the best one."* | *"photo by: her"* |
| 4–8 s | A rapid montage of B's terrible photos of A (café, mall, car). B appears beside her, unbothered. | B: *"You moved."* A: *"I was* sitting*."* | *"exhibit B" "exhibit C"* |
| 8–14 s | Cut to the rooftop at golden hour. A poses at the railing and B lazily snaps one without looking, mid-sentence. | B: *"…yeah, yeah, one more."* | — |
| 14–19 s | They look at the screen. It's stunning: backlit hair, sky on fire. They both stare in silence. | A, whispering: *"…Did you take this?"* | — |
| 19–23 s | B turns to camera, chin up, a different woman. | B: *"I've always been this talented."* | *"the light did it"* |
| 23–25 s | End frame. | — | *"#OPEN AIR · 05.12 · Regenta Rooftop"* |

**Caption:** *"#collab Tag your worst photographer. Bring her on the 5th. Even she could win the ₹10,000 video prize up here 😭"* That links to C50's real prize. Check that the rules allow any guest to enter.
**The final photo** goes in the carousel or Story as the "after". Use only the creators' own photos, never other guests'.

## R10 · "Ajji's first rooftop" · Trio (three generations) · 35 s
**Job:** the strongest trust move in the set. The oldest woman in the family gives permission. **Before:** "My family would never be okay with this." **After:** "If Ajji's blessing it, the family question is answered." **Frame:** identity and authority, the family kind.

**Cast:** the creator (granddaughter), her mother (Amma) and her grandmother (Ajji). They visit the rooftop in daylight on 3 Dec, before the event. **This does not claim Ajji is coming to the night.** It's a 21+ evening, and she's giving her blessing, not attending.

**Hooks written:**
1. ★ The lift doors open. Ajji, in a silk saree, grips her granddaughter's arm, steps out and stops still. *"Seventy-[age] years in Hubballi. I've never seen it from up here."* (story cold-open + emotion)
2. ★ The text *"I took my Ajji to the rooftop where Saturday's happening"* over her walking out of the lift (callout + curiosity)
3. *"Three generations. One rooftop. One verdict."* (format + stakes)
4. *"Asking my grandmother if I can go to a techno night."* (open loop; possibly too close to C30's "Asking Appa")
5. *"Ajji vs. the bass drop."* (humour; save it as a beat, not the hook)

| Time | Shot | Audio / line | On-screen text |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–4 s | **Hook.** POV from inside the lift: the doors part, Ajji steps out on the granddaughter's arm, sees the skyline and stops. | Ajji: *"Seventy-[age] years in Hubballi. I've never seen it from up here."* | *"Ajji's first rooftop"* |
| 4–8 s | She walks slowly to the railing and points. Amma stands beside her. | Ajji: *"That was all fields when I got married. And that road wasn't even there."* | — |
| 8–13 s | The granddaughter shows them the stage area and the women security staff, who come over and say hello to Ajji (with consent). | Granddaughter: *"This is where everyone comes in. They check everyone at the door."* Amma, quietly to Ajji: *"Women at the door."* | — |
| 13–19 s | The granddaughter puts an LED headphone set on Ajji. It glows orange. Ajji's face goes from suspicious to delighted as she nods to the beat. | Silence for the viewer, a faint thump. Ajji laughs out loud. | *"Ajji vs. techno"* |
| 19–24 s | Ajji takes the headphones off, all three sitting together as the sun goes down. | Ajji: *"In my time, we had to sneak to the movies."* Amma: *"Amma!"* | — |
| 24–31 s | Ajji turns to her granddaughter and holds her hand. | Ajji: *"You go on Saturday. Come home and tell me everything."* The granddaughter looks at Amma. Amma nods. | — |
| 31–33 s | All three, silhouetted against the sunset. | — | *"Permission granted. By the highest authority."* |
| 33–35 s | End frame. | — | *"#OPEN AIR · 05.12 · Regenta Rooftop"* |

**Caption:** *"#ad Took my Ajji where I'm going on Saturday. She took the headphones home in her heart 🤍"*
**Why it works:** R4 answers Amma's questions. R10 goes one step further: the oldest woman in the family *blesses* the night on camera. For a viewer whose barrier is the family conversation, that's the strongest proof this campaign can show.
**Consent:** written consent from Ajji and Amma, the security staff and Regenta. Keep Ajji comfortable: give her a chair, keep her well away from the edge, and shoot it in no more than about 20 minutes.
