# 11 · 5 rooftop scripts for women creators: 3 solo, 1 duo, 1 trio

**Bi Ki BOO presents #OPEN AIR · Regenta rooftop, Airport Road · Sat 5 Dec · Bullzeye**
All five are shot on the Regenta rooftop itself. They follow the specs in [10-video-briefs-55.md](10-video-briefs-55.md): 9:16, warm sunset grade, captions on, no bottles or labels, no guests' faces on the Terrace, and the end frame *"#OPEN AIR · 05.12 · Regenta Rooftop"*.

---

## The rule for these five: sell without selling
These videos never say "book", "tickets", "buy", "limited", "don't miss" or a price. The rooftop, the sunset and the people do the persuading. Each script uses one lever:

| # | Title | Cast | Length | Lever |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | **"Guess the city"** | Solo | 20 s | Pride: *"this is Hubballi?"* |
| R2 | **"Don't come home this weekend"** | Solo | 30 s | Longing: the friend who moved to Bengaluru |
| R3 | **"Mid-sentence"** | Solo | 15 s | Mystery: the 10 pm headphones |
| R4 | **"Amma's 7 voice notes"** | Duo | 30 s | Safety and trust, told as a joke |
| R5 | **"Green flags only"** | Trio | 35 s | Social proof: friends rating the rooftop like a first date |

**The first 8 seconds.** Every script opens on a face, a sound or a question. The place is never explained until the viewer is already hooked. The event name only shows in the last 2 seconds.

**Shoot slot.** 3 Dec, during the soundcheck preview, 4:45–6:15 pm, so you catch golden hour and the lights coming on. Shoot R3 after dark.
**Wardrobe.** Brand palette: black, ivory, burnt orange. Carry a shawl or blazer, because it's 16 °C up there and R2 and R4 both use it.
**Kannada lines** are written plainly. The creator should say them in her own Uttara Karnataka Kannada, not read them word for word.

## R1 · "Guess the city" · Solo · 20 s
*Rooftop edge at golden hour, skyline behind. Post 1 Dec.*

| Time | Shot | Audio / line | On-screen text |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–3 s | **Hook.** Tight on her face, wind in her hair, sun flare behind. She looks straight into the lens. | *"Guess maadi, naanu ellidini. Bengaluru anta helidre, block maadtini."* (Guess where I am. Say Bengaluru and I'll block you.) | *"Guess the city 👀"* |
| 3–8 s | Slow pull back. The skyline appears but it's still hard to place. She raises one eyebrow. | Approved track fades in, low. | Fake guesses pop up one by one: *"Dubai?" "Goa?" "Mumbai?"* |
| 8–14 s | She turns and points down at Airport Road. If you can time it, a plane is coming in to land at Hubballi airport. | *"Aa plane eega Hubballi airport ge land aaytu."* (That plane just landed at Hubballi airport.) | *"Hubballi. Airport Road."* |
| 14–18 s | She sits on a lounger and pulls her knees up. The sun touches the horizon. | *"Manege ten minutes."* (Ten minutes from home.) | — |
| 18–20 s | End frame over the sunset. | Track swells. | *"#OPEN AIR · 05.12 · Regenta Rooftop"* |

**Caption:** *"Wrong answers only. Best guess in the comments gets a pass from me 🤍"* Put the code in the Story sticker.
**Why it works:** the comment section does the promotion. Every wrong guess ("Dubai!") tells the next viewer that this looks like a world-class place, and Hubballi pride does the rest.

## R2 · "Don't come home this weekend" · Solo · 30 s
*Rooftop at golden hour, then the first lights. Post 2 Dec. Aimed at Hubballi women working in Bengaluru and Pune who come home on weekends.*

| Time | Shot | Audio / line | On-screen text |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–4 s | **Hook.** Close on the phone screen: a WhatsApp voice note recording to *"[Friend] 🏙️ Blr"*, the waveform moving. Her voice, hushed: | *"Hey. Don't come home this weekend."* | *"voice note to my best friend in Bengaluru"* |
| 4–8 s | Cut to her face. She laughs at herself and shakes her head. | *"Okay, that came out wrong. Come home. Just… don't make plans for Saturday evening."* | — |
| 8–15 s | She flips the camera. A slow pan across the skyline, sun low. | *"I'm standing somewhere I'm not allowed to tell you about yet."* | — |
| 15–22 s | Back on her, the shawl round her shoulders, the city behind her. Quieter now. | *"You always said Hubballi would never have something like this. That we'd have to leave to get it."* | — |
| 22–27 s | She looks out. The lighting rig comes on behind her, stage by stage. | *"Bring a jacket. It's sixteen degrees up here."* | — |
| 27–30 s | Back to the phone: she hits send, and the ✓✓ ticks turn blue. End frame. | Track rises. | *"#OPEN AIR · 05.12 · Regenta Rooftop"* |

**Caption:** *"If you're coming home this weekend, you know who this is for."* No code in this one. Pin the code in the comments if people ask.
**Why it works:** it doesn't sell to the viewer, it sells to *someone the viewer knows*, so people send it on. Instagram rewards sends more than likes.

## R3 · "Mid-sentence" · Solo · 15 s
*Rooftop after dark, the LED headphone rack behind her. Post 3 Dec night. Builds the 10 pm headphone mystery from C33 and C44. Don't explain it.*

| Time | Shot | Audio / line | On-screen text |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–3 s | **Hook.** She's already talking fast to camera, mid-rant, as if we walked in late. | *"…and honestly, the thing about Hubballi nights is that nobody ever—"* | — |
| 3–6 s | Without stopping, she slips on a pair of the LED headphones. They light up warm orange. | **The audio cuts to near silence.** Only a faint, muffled thump. | — |
| 6–10 s | She freezes and her eyes widen. She opens her mouth to finish the sentence and can't. A slow smile. | Muffled thump, a bit louder. | *"Saturday, 10 pm."* |
| 10–13 s | She lifts one ear cup off, about to explain, then thinks better of it, puts it back and turns away, starting to move. | Silence. | *"She still hasn't finished that sentence."* |
| 13–15 s | End frame over the glowing headphones. | — | *"#OPEN AIR · 05.12 · Regenta Rooftop"* |

**Caption:** *"I was going to explain. I can't."*
**Why it works:** the viewer is shut out of the sound she's hearing. That small sense of exclusion is the whole pitch. It loops well because the last frame leads back into the unfinished sentence.

## R4 · "Amma's 7 voice notes" · Duo · 30 s
*Two friends on the rooftop at golden hour. Post 2 Dec. This is the safety message for women and their parents, told so it never feels like a safety message.*

**Cast:** A (holds the phone) and B (does the showing). Amma is only a voice: record a real mother or aunt, with her consent.

| Time | Shot | Audio / line | On-screen text |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–3 s | **Hook.** A and B mid-selfie at sunset, laughing. A WhatsApp ping, then another, then five more. They both freeze. | *Ping ×7.* A: *"…Amma."* | *"Amma · 7 voice notes"* |
| 3–8 s | A plays the first one, holding the phone between them. B grins at camera. | **Amma:** *"Elli iddira?"* (Where are you two?) | Subtitle |
| 8–11 s | B flips the camera to the skyline. | A: *"Hubballi, Amma. Airport Road."* | — |
| 11–15 s | Voice note 2. B walks the camera to the door and the female security staff (with consent), who give a small wave. | **Amma:** *"Alli yaaryaaru iddare?"* (Who's there?) | *"✓ women on security"* |
| 15–18 s | Voice note 3. B holds up the event's safety card with the close time. | **Amma:** *"Tumba late aagatta?"* (Will it go very late?) | *"✓ closes at [close time]"* |
| 18–21 s | Voice note 4. B points at the help desk and the verified cab partner sign. | **Amma:** *"Vaapas hege bartira?"* (How will you come back?) | *"✓ help desk ✓ verified cabs"* |
| 21–24 s | Voice note 5. Both hold up their shawls at once, deadpan. | **Amma:** *"Chali ide, shawl tagondiddira?"* (It's cold, did you take shawls?) | *"✓ 16 °C, yes Amma"* |
| 24–28 s | Voice note 6, and a pause. They lean in. | **Amma:** *"…Naanu bartini."* (…I'm coming too.) | — |
| 28–30 s | They look at each other in silence, then burst out laughing. End frame. | Track kicks in. | *"Amma wants in."* → *"#OPEN AIR · 05.12 · Regenta Rooftop"* |

**Caption:** *"Tag the friend whose Amma sends 7 voice notes."*
**Why it works:** the biggest reason women in Hubballi don't go out is the conversation at home. This answers every question in that conversation (security, close time, cab, cold) while the viewer thinks she's watching a joke. The tag prompt gets it to friends, and the ending gets it to mothers.
**Consent:** Regenta, the security staff on camera, and whoever voices Amma.

## R5 · "Green flags only" · Trio · 35 s
*Three friends on loungers at golden hour, then the lights. Post 3 Dec. Never sit on the parapet or ledge.*

**Cast:** C (the host, asks the questions), D (the sceptic) and E (the hype one).

| Time | Shot | Audio / line | On-screen text |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–3 s | **Hook.** The three of them in a row, C in the middle, sunglasses on, judging-panel faces. | C: *"We're rating this rooftop like it's a first date."* | *"rating a rooftop like a first date"* |
| 3–8 s | D crosses her arms. | D: *"I've been on worse dates."* E: *"You've been on* this *date?"* D: *"…No."* | — |
| 8–12 s | **Flag 1.** Cut to the sun sinking behind the skyline. | C: *"Shows up on time. Every single evening."* | *"🟢 punctual"* |
| 12–16 s | **Flag 2.** A low-angle shot looking out over the city. | E: *"Tall. Like, the-whole-city-below-you tall."* | *"🟢 tall"* |
| 16–21 s | **Flag 3.** Cut to the booth (with Bullzeye's consent): the soundcheck bass hits and all three jump. | D, impressed despite herself: *"Okay… good taste in music."* | *"🟢 bringing Bullzeye"* |
| 21–25 s | **Flag 4.** A quick glance at the help desk and the female security staff. | C: *"Your parents would actually like him."* | *"🟢 parents approve"* |
| 25–29 s | **Flag 5.** All three again, shawls on, the lights coming up. | E: *"And he doesn't make you drive to Bengaluru."* | *"🟢 lives in Hubballi"* |
| 29–33 s | D lifts a finger. | D: *"One red flag. He's only free on the 5th."* C and E look at her. D shrugs: *"…I can work with that."* | *"🚩 only free Saturday"* |
| 33–35 s | End frame. | Track swells. | *"#OPEN AIR · 05.12 · Regenta Rooftop"* |

**Caption:** *"Rate him out of 10. We said 11."* Put the +3 squad pass code in the Story sticker.
**Why it works:** the sceptic is the viewer's stand-in. When D comes round, the viewer comes round with her. The "only free on the 5th" red flag gives the date and the urgency without anyone saying "limited".
