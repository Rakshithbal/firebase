# 11 · 5 rooftop scripts for women creators: 3 solo, 1 duo, 1 trio

**Bi Ki BOO presents #OPEN AIR · Regenta rooftop, Airport Road · Sat 5 Dec · Bullzeye**
All five are shot on the Regenta rooftop. They follow the specs in [10-video-briefs-55.md](10-video-briefs-55.md): 9:16, warm sunset grade, captions on, no bottles or labels, no guests' faces on the Terrace, and the end frame *"#OPEN AIR · 05.12 · Regenta Rooftop"*.

This version has been run through the Content Influence OS: a decision brief first, then each script tied to one belief it has to change, then a score out of 50 before anything is posted.

## Part 1 · Decision brief

| Field | Answer |
|---|---|
| **Audience** | Women aged 21–35 in Hubballi–Dharwad, plus Hubballi women working in Bengaluru or Pune who come home at weekends |
| **Awareness stage** | **Problem-aware.** They feel there's nowhere to go out here, but they don't know a night like this exists in their own city. So the first piece has to show the category exists ("there's a better way"), not push the event. |
| **Message saturation** | **Level 2 locally, Level 5 for the "party" promise.** "Rooftop party" and "the biggest night" are worn-out claims. Lead with **identity and experience** (pride, friendship, Amma) and the **mechanism** (sunset → drummers → Bullzeye → 10 pm headphones), never with hype. |
| **Decision equation** | *"A proper night out means Bengaluru, and it isn't really for girls like me here"* → *"There's one here, it's safe, my friends are going, and Amma would say yes"* → **use a creator's code to get on the guest list with my squad or partner before Saturday.** |
| **Primary barrier** | **Don't trust**: safety, what parents will say, "will there be other women?" Second: **don't care**: "techno isn't my thing." |
| **Frames** | Identity (R1, R2), Social (R5), Simplicity and reassurance (R4), Curiosity, an open loop that does get closed (R3) |

### Offer diagnostic: provisional pass, with three conditions

| Lever | Rating | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Dream outcome | Strong | A sunset rooftop with a national act, without leaving home |
| Perceived likelihood ("will it be okay for *me*?") | **Weakest lever** | Depends entirely on the safety setup being real and visible. R4 exists for this. |
| Time delay | Strong | One evening, 10 minutes from the city |
| Effort | Strong | Cabs, a Belagavi shuttle, a fixed close time |
| Risk reversal | Medium | Help desk, verified cabs, women on security. There's no refund or weather policy in the briefs I have. |
| Value stack | **Unknown** | I don't have the entry price or what a pass includes |
| Real urgency | Strong, *if true* | One date is a real deadline. "It's full" or "limited" claims are only allowed if capacity numbers back them. |

**Before posting, confirm:** (1) the price and entry terms in the caption link, (2) that women security staff, the help desk, the verified cab partner and the close time will all be in place on 5 Dec, because R4 promises them on camera, and (3) real capacity numbers before any "full" language, including C45.

## Part 3 · The scripts

### R1 · "Guess the city" · Solo · 20 s
**Job:** show the category exists here. **Before:** "This kind of place doesn't exist in Hubballi." **After:** "It does, and it's ours." **Frame:** identity and pride.

**Hooks written (top 2 kept ★):**
1. ★ *"Guess where I am. Say Bengaluru and I'll block you."* (callout + curiosity)
2. ★ Silent: a face in the wind, and only the text *"Guess the city. Wrong answers only."* (pattern interrupt)
3. *"This view costs an 8-hour drive. Or ten minutes."* (contrast)
4. *"My cousin in Dubai thinks this is Dubai."* (specificity, but only use it if it's true)
5. *"POV: you find out where you are."* (open loop)

| Time | Shot | Audio / line | On-screen text |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–3 s | **Hook.** Tight on her face, wind in her hair, sun flare. Straight into the lens. | *"Guess where I am. Say Bengaluru and I'll block you."* | *"Guess the city 👀"* |
| 3–8 s | Slow pull back. The skyline appears but is still hard to place. One eyebrow up. | Approved track fades in, low. | Guesses pop up: *"Dubai?" "Goa?" "Mumbai?"* |
| 8–13 s | She turns and points down at Airport Road. A plane comes in to Hubballi airport (time it). | *"That plane just landed at Hubballi airport."* | *"Hubballi. Airport Road."* |
| 13–18 s | She sits, knees up, as the sun touches the horizon. | *"Bengaluru is eight hours away. Home is ten minutes. This view is ours."* | — |
| 18–20 s | End frame over the sunset. | Track swells. | *"#OPEN AIR · 05.12 · Regenta Rooftop"* |

**Caption:** *"#collab Wrong answers only 🤍 I'll pick my favourite guess at 9 pm on 3 Dec and give them a pass."* Code goes in the Story sticker. The giveaway has to be real, and the winner announced in Stories.

### R2 · "Don't come home this weekend" · Solo · 30 s
**Job:** name the problem and its cause. **Before:** "To get this, you have to leave." **After:** "You don't have to any more." **Frame:** identity and longing. Built to be sent to the friend who left.

**Hooks written:**
1. ★ A voice note recording on screen: *"Hey. Don't come home this weekend."* (contrarian + open loop)
2. ★ *"Voice note to my best friend who moved to Bengaluru"* as text over the waveform (callout)
3. *"You said we'd have to leave to get this."* (story cold-open)
4. *"Sending this to [friend] before she books her bus."* (specificity + stakes)
5. *"If your best friend lives in Bengaluru, send her this."* (callout; save this one for the caption)

| Time | Shot | Audio / line | On-screen text |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–4 s | **Hook.** Close on the phone: a WhatsApp voice note recording to *"[Friend] 🏙️ Blr"*, waveform moving. Hushed: | *"Hey. Don't come home this weekend."* | *"voice note to my best friend in Bengaluru"* |
| 4–8 s | Her face. She laughs at herself. | *"Okay, that came out wrong. Come home. Just… don't make plans for Saturday evening."* | — |
| 8–14 s | She flips the camera for a slow skyline pan, sun low. | *"I'm standing somewhere. I'll tell you when you land."* | — |
| 14–21 s | Back on her, shawl on, city behind. Quieter. | *"You always said Hubballi would never have something like this. That we'd have to leave to get it."* | — |
| 21–25 s | She holds up the black invite card against the sky. The city lights come on below. | *"Bring a jacket. It's sixteen degrees up here."* | — |
| 25–28 s | She hits send, and the ✓✓ turn blue. **A reply voice note arrives straight away** (the friend's real voice, with her consent). | **Friend:** *"…Send location."* | — |
| 28–30 s | She grins. End frame. | Track rises. | *"#OPEN AIR · 05.12 · Regenta Rooftop"* |

**Caption:** *"#ad If you're coming home this weekend, you know who this is for."* Pin the code in the comments.
**What changed after scoring:** the old line *"somewhere I'm not allowed to tell you about"* was dropped, because it was a false secret and hurt credibility. The reply *"Send location"* was added so the decision happens on screen, and the viewer sees the action she's meant to take.

### R3 · "Mid-sentence" · Solo (+1) · 15 s, plus a 4 Dec Story
**Job:** create curiosity about the mechanism, the 10 pm moment. **Before:** "It's just another DJ night." **After:** "Something happens at 10 that I have to be there for." **Frame:** a curiosity gap. **The loop must be closed** in a Story on 4 Dec, because an open loop that never closes loses trust.

**Hooks written:**
1. ★ Mid-rant, as if we walked in late: *"…and honestly, the thing about Hubballi nights is nobody ever—"* (pattern interrupt + story cold-open)
2. ★ The headphones light up, the audio cuts to silence, and the text reads *"turn your sound up. it won't help."* (pattern interrupt)
3. *"I can't tell you what happens at 10 pm. I can show you my face."* (open loop)
4. *"This is the only song in Hubballi you can't hear."* (contrarian)
5. *"She put these on and stopped talking."* (third-person curiosity)

| Time | Shot | Audio / line | On-screen text |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–3 s | **Hook.** Talking fast to camera, mid-rant, the headphone rack glowing behind her. | *"…and honestly, the thing about Hubballi nights is that nobody ever—"* | — |
| 3–6 s | Without stopping, she slips on the LED headphones. They glow orange. | **The audio cuts to near silence.** A faint, muffled thump. | — |
| 6–10 s | She freezes and her eyes widen. Her friend steps into frame, also in glowing headphones, already moving. They lock eyes and smile. | The thump gets a little louder. | *"Saturday, 10 pm."* |
| 10–13 s | She lifts one ear cup to explain, thinks better of it, puts it back and turns away to dance with her friend. | Silence. | *"She still hasn't finished that sentence."* |
| 13–15 s | End frame over the two glowing headphones. | — | *"#OPEN AIR · 05.12 · Regenta Rooftop"* |

**Caption:** *"#collab I was going to explain. I can't. Answer in my Stories tomorrow."*
**4 Dec Story (closes the loop):** a 10-second clip saying what the 10 pm moment is: *[production to confirm the format, e.g. the headphone segment, how many headphones, how long]*. End on *"Now you know. Saturday."*

### R4 · "Amma's 7 voice notes" · Duo · 30 s
**Job:** remove the trust barrier, for her *and* her parents. **Before:** "Amma will never say yes to a night thing." **After:** "Every question Amma has has an answer, and she'd want to come too." **Frame:** simplicity and reassurance, told as a joke.

**Cast:** A (holds the phone) and B (does the showing). Amma is a voice only: a real mother or aunt, with her consent.

**Hooks written:**
1. ★ Seven WhatsApp pings in a row. Both freeze. *"…Amma."* (pattern interrupt + relatability)
2. ★ The text *"Amma · 7 voice notes"* over two friends mid-selfie (curiosity)
3. *"We answered every one of Amma's questions with the camera."* (open loop)
4. *"Things Amma asks when you say 'rooftop'."* (callout)
5. *"How to get a yes from an Indian mother in 30 seconds."* (stakes; risks feeling like a lecture)

| Time | Shot | Audio / line | On-screen text |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–3 s | **Hook.** A and B mid-selfie at sunset, laughing. A WhatsApp ping, then another, then five more. Both freeze. | *Ping ×7.* A: *"…Amma."* | *"Amma · 7 voice notes"* |
| 3–8 s | A plays the first one between them. B grins at camera. | **Amma:** *"Where are you two?"* | Subtitle |
| 8–11 s | B flips the camera to the skyline. | A: *"Hubballi, Amma. Airport Road."* | — |
| 11–15 s | Voice note 2. The camera finds the women security staff at the door (with consent), who give a small wave. | **Amma:** *"Who else is there?"* | *"✓ women on security"* |
| 15–18 s | Voice note 3. B holds up the event's safety card: the close time and Regenta's name. | **Amma:** *"Will it go very late?"* | *"✓ closes at [close time]"* |
| 18–21 s | Voice note 4. B points at the help desk and the **[cab partner name]** sign. | **Amma:** *"How are you getting back?"* | *"✓ help desk ✓ [cab partner] cabs"* |
| 21–24 s | Voice note 5. Both hold up their shawls at once, deadpan. | **Amma:** *"It's cold. Did you take your shawls?"* | *"✓ 16 °C, yes Amma"* |
| 24–28 s | Voice note 6, and a pause. They lean in. | **Amma:** *"…I'm coming too."* | — |
| 28–30 s | They look at each other in silence, then burst out laughing. End frame. | Track kicks in. | *"Amma wants in."* → *"#OPEN AIR · 05.12 · Regenta Rooftop"* |

**Caption:** *"#ad Tag the friend whose Amma sends 7 voice notes. Or forward this to Amma directly 😌"* The Story link goes to a one-page **parent card** with the safety details.
**What changed after scoring:** the cab partner is now **named** (independent validation, proof level 4–5) instead of "verified cab". *"Forward this to Amma"* was added, which gives the video a second audience and a second job.
**Every ✓ on screen must be true on 5 Dec.** If something changes, re-edit the video before it goes out.

**Hooks written:**
1. ★ *"We're rating this rooftop like it's a first date."* (pattern interrupt + format promise)

3. *"Our friend refused to come. Watch what changed her mind."* (open loop, but it gives away the ending)
4. *"Green flags only. Except one."* (curiosity)
5. *"Would you date this rooftop?"* (question callout)

| 8–12 s | **Flag 1.** The sun sinking behind the skyline. | C: *"Shows up on time. Every single evening."* | *"🟢 punctual"* |
| 12–16 s | **Flag 2.** A low angle out over the city. | E: *"Tall. Like, the-whole-city-below-you tall."* | *"🟢 tall"* |

| 21–25 s | **Flag 4.** A quick look at the help desk and the women security staff. | C: *"Your parents would actually like him."* | *"🟢 parents approve"* |
| 25–29 s | **Flag 5.** Shawls on, the lights coming up. | E: *"And he doesn't make you drive to Bengaluru."* | *"🟢 lives in Hubballi"* |

**Caption:** *"#collab Rate him out of 10. We said 11."* The +3 squad pass code goes in the Story sticker.
