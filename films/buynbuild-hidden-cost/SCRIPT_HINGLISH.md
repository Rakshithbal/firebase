# ₹5 lakh jo kisi ne nahi bataya — Hinglish script (DRAFT for approval)

Same 83 lines, same facts and same cautious wording as the approved English script. Nothing new added.
Read by ElevenLabs "Bhavesh" at about 140 words per minute.
On-screen text (stamps, cards, the LEAK METER, the checklist) stays in English, as in the reference style.

The voice gets the Hindi words in Devanagari so he pronounces them correctly. The captions use this Roman Hinglish.

## 1 · Cold open

1. Karnataka ki ek family contractor se ek simple sawaal poochhti hai: hamara ghar kitne ka banega?
2. Woh kehta hai, pachchees lakh rupaye.
3. Atthaarah mahine baad, unka kharcha ho chuka hai lagbhag tees lakh.
4. Ab ajeeb baat suniye. Kisi ne unse jhooth nahi bola. Koi paisa lekar bhaaga nahi.
5. Toh woh paanch lakh rupaye gaye kahaan?
6. Aaj hum ek-ek rupaya dhoondhenge. Total saat leaks hain.
7. Aur leak number saat cement ya steel ka nahi hai. Woh kaagaz ka hai, aur woh aapka bank loan ek jhatke mein rok sakta hai.
8. End tak rukiyega, kyunki hum aapko woh chhe sawaal denge jo har leak band kar dete hain.

## 2 · The quote

9. India mein har ghar ka quote ek hi formula se shuru hota hai.
10. Built-up area, guna rate per square foot.
11. Ek hazaar square feet, dhaai hazaar rupaye per square foot. Pachchees lakh.
12. Basic ghar aksar atthaarah sau se teis sau rupaye per square foot ke aas-paas quote hote hain.
13. Standard, lagbhag untees sau tak. Premium, usse upar.
14. Yeh number aapko ghar ki keemat lagta hai.
15. Par hai nahi. Yeh sirf us cheez ki keemat hai jo contractor ke rate ke andar aati hai. Aur aapke ghar ka bahut saara hissa uske bahar rehta hai.

## 3 · Leak 1 — The area

16. Leak number ek chhupa hai ek shabd mein: area.
17. Contractor aam taur pe built-up area pe quote karte hain: har floor, deewaron, balcony aur seedhiyon ke saath.
18. Lekin aap rehte ho carpet area mein, woh floor jis pe aap sach mein chal sakte ho. Aur woh hamesha chhota hota hai.
19. Toh jab do quote same rate bataayein, poochhiye: rate kaunse area pe? Jawaab likhit mein lijiye.
20. Chhoti baat lagti hai. Poore ghar pe, total lakhon mein badal jaata hai.

## 4 · Leak 2 — The list

21. Leak number do sabse bada hai, aur woh ek list hai.
22. Plan approval fees. Khudaai se pehle plan sanction hona chahiye, aur uska paisa lagta hai.
23. Soil test aur structural design. Kisi ko zameen check karni padegi, aur us pe khada hone wala design karna padega.
24. Compound wall aur gate. Borewell, sump aur overhead tank.
25. Har plot ko inki zaroorat hai. Par inmein se koi bhi kamra nahi hai, isliye koi bhi rate per square foot mein fit nahi hota.
26. Bijli aur paani ke connection.
27. Inke bina ghar mein reh hi nahi sakte. Phir bhi yeh rate ke bahar hain.
28. Interiors: modular kitchen, wardrobes. Woh cheezein jo makaan ko ghar banaati hain.
29. Aur GST, agar woh quote mein pehle se nahi tha.
30. Inmein se koi bhi dhokha nahi hai. Bas yeh per square foot rate ke andar nahi aate.
31. Aur zyaadatar quotes inka zikr hi nahi karte, toh koi inhe ginta nahi, jab tak bills nahi aate.
32. Hamare example mein, yeh milkar lagbhag teen lakh ho jaate hain.
33. Teen lakh, jo quote mein kabhi tha hi nahi.
34. Aur phir woh paisa jo har builder ko chahiye, par almost koi budget nahi karta: contingency.
35. Aam taur pe paanch se das percent, mehngaai ke liye, aur un badlaavon ke liye jo aap raaste mein karoge.
36. Kyunki badlaav aap karoge. Yahaan thodi badi khidki. Wahaan thodi achhi tiles.
37. Aath percent pe, yeh hai aur do lakh chaubees hazaar.
38. Pachchees lakh ab ban gaye tees lakh.

## 5 · Leak 3 — The soil

39. Ab woh leaks jo aapko tees ke bhi paar le ja sakte hain.
40. Leak number teen hai mitti.
41. North Karnataka mein kaafi zameen black cotton soil pe hai. Geeli hone pe phoolti hai, sookhne pe sikudti hai.
42. Isse normal zameen samajhkar banaaoge, toh cracks aayenge. Sahi tareeke se banaaoge, toh foundation mehnga padega.
43. Isliye soil test sirf kaagazi kaarvaai nahi hai. Yeh poore project ka sabse sasta insurance hai.

## 6 · Leak 4 — The bag and the bar

44. Leak number chaar chupchaap hota hai, material yard pe.
45. Koi kuch chhupa nahi raha. Bas quote vague hai.
46. Quote mein likha hai cement. Par kaunsa cement? 33, 43 ya 53 grade? OPC ya PPC?
47. PPC aam taur pe per bag thoda sasta hota hai, aur sahi use ho toh dono theek hain.
48. Quote mein likha hai steel. Par kaunsa TMT? Fe 500 ya 500D? Kaunsa brand?
49. Har choice se price badalta hai. Aur jo quote sirf cement aur steel likhta hai, woh nahi batata ki aapko kaunsa mil raha hai.
50. Aur baat cement aur steel pe nahi rukti. Tiles, paint, wires aur pipes, sab sasti aur mehngi dono quality mein aate hain.
51. Ek baar deewar ke andar ya floor ke neeche chale gaye, toh sab ek jaise dikhte hain. Jab tak inmein se koi fail na ho jaaye.
52. Agar quote mein brand aur grade ka naam nahi hai, toh do contractor same price mein do bilkul alag ghar quote kar sakte hain.
53. Kaagaz pe same rate. Par ghar same nahi.
54. Isliye itemised quote maangiye: cement, steel, tiles, paint, wiring aur plumbing ka brand aur grade.
55. Line by line, taaki har number ke saath uska naam likha ho.
56. Ab aap same ghar compare kar rahe ho.

## 7 · Leak 5 — Paid twice

57. Leak number paanch sabse dardnaak hai, kyunki iska paisa aap do baar dete ho.
58. Jab concrete ko theek se vibrate nahi kiya jaata, toh usmein gaps aur khaali jagah reh jaati hai. Engineers ise honeycombing kehte hain.
59. Aur jo concrete bahut jaldi sookh jaata hai, woh kabhi apni poori strength tak nahi pahunchta.
60. Isliye usse cure karna padta hai, kai din tak geela rakhna padta hai.
61. India ka concrete code ordinary cement ke saath kam se kam saat din maangta hai, aur PPC jaise blended cement ke saath das din.
62. Isse skip kiya, toh repair ka bill baad mein aata hai. Byaaj ke saath.

## 8 · Leak 6 — The white wall

63. Leak number chhe ghar mein shift hone ke ek saal baad dikhta hai.
64. Deewaron pe safed, powder jaise dhabbe. Ise efflorescence kehte hain: namak, jo nami ke saath upar aata hai.
65. Plinth level pe ek patla damp-proof course us nami ko rokne mein madad karta hai.
66. Ise chhod diya, toh aap wahi deewar baar-baar paint karte rahoge.

## 9 · Leak 7 — Paper

67. Aur ab, leak number saat. Woh jo kaagaz ka bana hai.
68. Karnataka mein khata batata hai ki aapki property record mein hai.
69. A-khata pe banks loan dene mein sabse zyada comfortable hote hain. B-khata properties ko loan aur approvals mein dikkat aa sakti hai.
70. Bengaluru mein ab property register karne ke liye e-khata chahiye.
71. Phir aata hai sanctioned plan. Aur end mein, occupancy certificate.
72. Kai shehron mein permanent connections dene se pehle yeh maanga jaata hai.
73. Kaagaz galat hua, toh duniya ka sabse sasta ghar bhi woh ban sakta hai jis pe loan hi na mile.
74. Na bech sakte ho. Na poora kar sakte ho.

## 10 · The fix

75. Toh yeh rahe woh chhe sawaal jo har leak band karte hain.
76. Ek: rate built-up area pe hai ya carpet area pe?
77. Do: is rate mein kya shaamil nahi hai?
78. Teen: kya soil test hua hai?
79. Chaar: har material ka brand aur grade kya hai?
80. Paanch: concrete aur curing kaun check karega?
81. Chhe: kya khata aur plan sanction clear hain?
82. Sign karne se pehle yeh poochh lijiye, aur pachchees lakh, pachchees lakh ke kareeb hi rahega.
