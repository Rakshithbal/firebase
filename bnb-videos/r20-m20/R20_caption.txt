Your engineer says "use M20". What does that mean?

M stands for mix. The number is the strength grade the concrete is designed to reach, checked by testing small cubes. Your engineer picks the grade for each part of your house.

On site, the grade only holds if the mix is right. Extra water makes it easier to pour, but weaker.

Ask which grade goes where, and how the mix is checked.
