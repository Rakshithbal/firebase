That thin crack on your terrace looks harmless in summer. In the monsoon, water can seep through it into the slab and show up as damp patches on the ceiling below.

Check it now, while it is dry: parapet walls, around pipes, and where water stands after rain. Mark each crack with chalk, take a photo, and show your engineer before the rains.

Save this for your pre-monsoon check. Send it to whoever looks after your terrace.
