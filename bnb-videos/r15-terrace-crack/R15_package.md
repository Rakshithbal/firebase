## 4. Animation notes for the editor

The whole reel is code. To change something, edit the scene files, not a timeline.

- **Files:** `index.html` (grid background, series tag, captions, 0.28 s cross-fades), `lib.js` (palette `P`, helpers `L`, same as R05), `scenes/common.js` (R15 helpers: sun, cloud, rain, crack generator, chalk loop, magnifier, engineer icon, phone, bookmark, ring/tag pops), `scenes/s1.js` to `s7.js` (one scene each), `timing.json` (master clock).
- **Timing source of truth is `timing.json`.** Every hit is tied to a spoken word with `L.wordTime(T, 'L0x', 'word')`. If the VO is ever re-voiced, regenerate `timing.json` and the visuals and SFX (`make_audio.py` reads the same file) follow on their own. Only the scene start/end boundaries are hard-coded, at the mid-points of the gaps between lines.
- **Deterministic:** no `Math.random` or `Date`. All texture, rain and crack shapes come from the seeded `L.rng`, so a re-render gives identical frames.
- **Key sync points (s):** magnifier lands on "crack" 0.94 · LOOKS HARMLESS 2.11 · sun pulse "summer" 3.07 · MONSOON 4.26 · drops hit 5.15 / 5.35 / 5.54 · WATER SEEPS IN 6.35 · SLAB 7.69 · damp-patch callout 9.26 · push-in "Over" 11.87 · STEEL INSIDE 13.90 · RUST ≈14.5 · REPAIRS GET BIGGER 15.76 · CHECK / IT / NOW 17.43 / 17.82 / 18.03 · WHILE IT'S DRY 19.12 · footprints 19.89 to 21.8 · rings 21.81 / 23.08 / 24.07 · WATER STANDS 24.44 · chalk loops 25.84 to 27.01 · shutter 28.42 · photo card 29.48 · engineer 30.05 · BEFORE THE RAINS 31.01 · "?" 31.73 · branch 31.96 · SEAL IT 32.87 · CLOSER LOOK 34.29 · bookmark 35.51.
- **Palette:** navy `#0E2A47`, chalk `#EAF2FF`, water `#4FC3F7`, crack red `#FF5A5A`, warning orange `#FF8A3D`, OK green `#7CE0A3`, sun `#FFB547`.
- **Safe area:** scene content stays inside x 60 to 1020 and y 230 to 1340. The s2 push-in and s5 close-up are clipped to that rectangle. Captions sit at y≈1418 to 1522 and the series tag at y 175.
- **No numbers on screen.** The cracks have no width, the rust ring has no scale, and there's no time or cost anywhere.

## 7. Music, pacing and subtitles

- **Music:** original, synthesized in `audio/make_audio.py`. No samples, no downloads. It uses the same felt-piano, pad and soft-pluck palette as R05 so the account sounds consistent. D major, ≈84.4 BPM (bar = 2.845 s).
  - Bars 0 to 1: bright Dmaj9 on the sunny terrace.
  - "Monsoon" (4.26 s) lands on Bm7, and the bass enters there.
  - Bars 2 to 5 (seepage and steel) are darker and held (Em9, Bm7, Gmaj7, Asus4 to A), with a quiet pluck pulse.
  - The **lift lands exactly on 17.07 s** ("So check it now"): flowing piano 8ths, a soft kick and a shaker under the checklist.
  - A rolled Dmaj9 resolves at 37.60 s, just after "check.", and the music fades to silence at 38.95 s.
- **Ambience:** synthesized rain bed comes in on "monsoon" and eases off by 11.9 s as the camera pushes into the slab, so the steel moment is quiet.
- **SFX (subtle, all synthesized):** lens swell + tick on "crack". Soft distant rumble on "monsoon" (no thunder crack). Three water plinks as drops hit the crack. A bubbly trickle on "seeps", a drip on "ceiling", a whoosh on the push-in, a soft metal ping on "steel". Pops and in-key chimes on tags and rings, soft footsteps across the plan, chalk scribbles, a phone shutter on "photo", a paper flick for the photo card, and a bookmark pop on "Save".
- **Mix/master:** voice HPF 70 Hz + 2.5:1 compression. Music bed -11 LU under the voice, ducked 9 dB under speech (60 ms down, 450 ms up). Rain -15 LU, ducked with the music. SFX -17 LU. Master: **-14.0 LUFS integrated, -2.0 dBTP limiter ceiling (MP4 measures -1.8 dBFS peak after AAC), LRA 1.3 LU**, 48 kHz stereo, 38.950 s.
- **Pacing:** a scene change or visual hit at least every 1 to 2 s. The longest single scene (s2, 11 s) is split into two beats by the push-in at 11.87 s.
- **Burned-in subtitles:** word by word from `timing.json`, Inter 800, 58 px, centred at y≈1470 on a dark rounded plate (78%). Chunks are 4 words or fewer and break at punctuation. The spoken word is orange, words already spoken are white, and upcoming words are dimmed. Turn Instagram auto-captions off to avoid double captions. Captions are verbatim to the VO, including "pre-monsoon".
