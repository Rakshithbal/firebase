Encumbrance certificate before paying a token for a property? Here's what to check when buying a plot, flat or house in Hubballi-Dharwad and Karnataka.

An encumbrance certificate (EC) is a record of registered transactions on a property, like sales or mortgages, for the period you ask for. It can show you if the property is already mortgaged, or has been sold before.

Before any token advance moves:
- Ask the seller for the encumbrance certificate first
- Check the owner's name and the property details match your sale agreement
- If something doesn't match, or the record has gaps, stop and ask why
- Show the EC and the agreement to your advocate

General guidance, not legal advice. Confirm with your advocate.
