## 7. Music, voice and credits

- **Voice:** AI voice, Kokoro (offline) `am_michael`, speed 1.0, via `tools/make_vo_voice.py`. Disclose as AI voice if the platform asks.
- **Music:** original, synthesized in `tools/notebook/make_audio.py` (no samples, no downloads): pizzicato upright-bass walk, woody staccato reed, shaker (Lemo-Opuscar `pluck.py` physical models for bass/harp/music box). G major loop G–Em–C–D at ≈98 BPM, tempo set so **the IN WRITING slam lands on a downbeat** (19.55 s). The bed gates out over the beat before the slam, then a soft kick + low bass + harp chord hit. A short harp arpeggio marks the first orange stroke on each page. The end card resolves on a tonic arpeggio + music box.
- **SFX:** generated from the page's own events (`ev.json`, dumped by `render.mjs --ev`): felt-tip hiss per stroke, glyph-by-glyph lettering, softer highlighter, tick squeak, page-flip swish + flap, rubber-stamp thump on the slam.
- **Mix/master:** voice HPF 70 Hz + 2.5:1 compression; music -12 LU under voice, ducked 10 dB under speech; SFX -16 LU; mastered to -14 LUFS with a -2 dBTP limiter. MP4: **-14.1 LUFS, -1.7 dBTP**.
- **Fonts:** Kalam, Inter, Bebas Neue (SIL OFL 1.1, see `style-samples/fonts/LICENSES.md`). **Logo:** `brand/bnb_mark_orange.png`, `brand/bnb_logo_orange.png`.

**Rebuild:** `sh tools/notebook/build.sh r08-written-quote R08_written_quote_michael G` (about 2.5 min: events → audio → 3 parallel JPG render chunks → encode → frames deleted → contact sheet). Stills: `NODE_PATH=$(npm root -g) node tools/notebook/render.mjs r08-written-quote --stills 3.6,20.2,27.6 --out <dir>`.
