Starting house construction without a written quote? That's this week's mistake, for anyone planning home building in Hubballi-Dharwad.

A handshake and a number on the phone feel quicker. Then the bill arrives, the number has changed, or the material isn't what you thought. Without paper, it's your memory against theirs.

Before work starts, ask your contractor for a written quote that shows:
- what work is included
- which materials, and which grade or type
- how payments are tied to construction stages

Read it, ask about anything missing, and keep a signed copy.
