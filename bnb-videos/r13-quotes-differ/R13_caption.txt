Why do construction quotes for the same house differ so much? Ask BNB, for families building a home in Hubballi-Dharwad.

Usually, because the quotes aren't for the same house. One contractor quote may include materials, another only labour. One may assume a different type of steel or cement, or leave out plastering, plumbing or electricals.

So don't compare totals. Compare line by line. Ask each contractor to quote the same list of work and materials, from the same drawing. Then the difference starts to make sense.
