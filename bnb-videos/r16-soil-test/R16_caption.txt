Soil test before foundation design: skipping it to save money is this week's home construction mistake. For anyone building a house in Hubballi-Dharwad and Karnataka.

Your foundation sits on the soil, and the soil decides how the foundation should be designed. Some soils are firm. Some, like black cotton soil, swell when wet and shrink when dry.

Design the foundation without knowing, and it can end up too weak, or costlier than it needs to be. And cracks that show up years later are much harder to fix.

Before your foundation is designed, ask your engineer:
- Do we need a soil test here?
- What will it tell us?

A small step, before the biggest part of your house.

General guidance. Confirm with your structural engineer.
