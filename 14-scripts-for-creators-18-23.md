# 14 · Scripts for women creators aged 18–23, across four hotel locations

**Bi Ki BOO presents #OPEN AIR · Regenta, Airport Road · Sat 5 Dec, 4 PM onwards · STOKED with Vinzula, Sachin and Blacout**
This is a shortlist of the 15 scripts in [11](11-rooftop-scripts-women.md), [12](12-rooftop-scripts-women-set2.md) and [13](13-rooftop-voiceover-scripts.md), picked and rewritten for **women creators aged 18–23**. Some now use the **lobby, pool or garden** instead of the rooftop. All other specs and rules from those files still apply.

---

## ⚠️ Read this first: entry age decides who can be cast
The C27 brief describes the crowd as **"21+"**, and Karnataka's legal drinking age is 21. A creator who is 18–20 saying *"see you Saturday"* is inviting her followers (mostly her own age) to an event they may not be allowed into. That's an ASCI problem and a trust problem.

| If entry is… | Cast |
|---|---|
| **21+** (assumed until confirmed) | Use **creators aged 21–23 only** for all ten scripts below. |
| **18+**, with separate wristbands for under-21s and no drinks served to them | 18–23 is fine, but 18–20 creators must never be shown near the bar or the drink shot (C46). |

**Confirm the entry age with the organiser before casting anyone under 21.**

## Location rules

| Location | Rules |
|---|---|
| **Lobby** | Shoot off-peak (2–4 pm) with Regenta's approval. No other hotel guests' faces: blur them or keep them out of frame. Don't film the check-in screens. |
| **Pool** | Book a 30-minute **closed pool** slot at golden hour, with the lifeguard present. **Creators stay dressed**: event outfits, feet in the water at most. No swimwear-led framing, because this is a nightlife promo aimed at a young audience. No running on the wet edge. |
| **Garden** | Golden hour, 4:30–5:45 pm. Watch for sprinklers and mosquitoes, and carry repellent. |
| **Rooftop** | Same as before. Never sit on the ledge or parapet. |

**Check with Regenta that the property has a pool and a garden you can use.** I'm assuming it does. If not, R9 and R5 go back to the rooftop as originally written, and R11, R15 and R7 move to a lounge or terrace.

**Every script still ends on, or points to, the rooftop.** The event is up there, so the other spaces are the way in.

## R14 · "What if" · Duo VO · 30 s · Lobby → lift
**What changed:** it's set during the walk in, when overthinking really happens. They walk through the lobby to the lift, and the doors opening is the answer. I added an exam line for the 18–23 audience, because early December is exam season.

| 17–22 s | **Lift** | They step in and the doors close. A, quieter now, watches the floor numbers climb. | **A:** *"What if… it's actually really good?"* |

| 27–30 s | **Rooftop** | B finally looks up at her. End frame. | **B:** *"…Yeah. That's the scary one."* |
